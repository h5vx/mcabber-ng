
 /OMEMO [info|own|on|off|default|trust|distrust|forget|fetch|cleanup] [jid] [device|fingerprint]

OMEMO end-to-end encryption of one-to-one chats (XEP-0384, compatible with Conversations, Dino, Gajim, Profanity...).  Rooms are not supported.
When the jid is omitted, the current buddy is used.  A device is designated by its ID or by its fingerprint (at least its first 8 digits).

/omemo info [jid]
 Show whether messages to jid are encrypted, and its devices with their trust level and fingerprint (without jid: this device)
/omemo own
 Show this device and the other devices of your account
/omemo on [jid]
 Always encrypt messages to jid (never send them unencrypted)
/omemo off [jid]
 Never encrypt messages to jid
/omemo default [jid]
 Follow the "omemo_policy" option for jid
/omemo trust [jid] device|fingerprint
 Mark the device as verified (compare the fingerprint with the contact first)
/omemo distrust [jid] device|fingerprint
 Never encrypt for this device, and refuse its messages
/omemo forget [jid] device|fingerprint
 Forget the keys and the session of the device
/omemo fetch [jid]
 Fetch the device list and the keys of jid again
/omemo cleanup
 Publish a device list that contains only this device (removes old devices of your account)
